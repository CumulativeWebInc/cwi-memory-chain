What this proves

Each anchor is an EIP-712 attestation: who signed, what blob hash it binds, and its link in the chain. This app re-checks the math on your phone — schema, UID, signature recovery, chain links.
Memory contents stay AES-256 encrypted in the private repo; only hashes and signatures are public. Blob-to-ciphertext binding is covered by the 35-check server suite in the private repo.
